---

## Branch 1 — TERRAIN *(know the ground before you move)*

- **Ornament: "The org chart feels it before the codebase does"** *(#3)*
  - Disruption lands on the intermediation layer — requirements, scrum masters, coordination PMs — not developers
  - Roles invert, not vanish: requirements → spec engineering · scrum → flow management
  - Light: ai-chezmoi — 34.5K LOC against a frozen spec; *the spec was the labor*
- **Ornament: "Capacity restoration, not headcount justification"** *(#4 — THE ARROW)*
  - Post-RIF, unionized, morale-scorched: AI will be read as "automating us to justify the cuts" unless framed as *helping the survivors carry the load*
  - Fire once. One sentence. As concern for the workforce, never commentary on the cuts
- **Ornament: "Rungs, not switches"** *(#2)*
  - Ladder: chat → copilot → agentic. Train to the person's current rung
  - Two-speed risk: fast/slow divergence breeds resentment → convert the fast into teachers
  - Light: you climbed every rung, with dates — and taught 3 to self-serve

## Branch 2 — TARGETING *(where & how, never "everyone")*

- **Ornament: "Tools don't transform; workflows do"** *(#1 — the Copilot Fallacy)*
  - Seat licenses plateau at autocomplete → "AI is overhyped" → program dies untried
  - Light: your own 14 months of Copilot (modest) vs 4 months agentic (measured 3–5×). Same person; the workflow was the variable
- **Ornament: "WHERE before HOW — the task-shape screen"** *(#6)*
  - AI-leverageable = verifiable output · assemblable context · cheap iteration · high volume or chronically deferred
  - First targets: legacy intent recovery · documentation debt · test coverage · ATO evidence
  - Anti-targets: irreversible calls · sparse-context judgment · PII before governance
  - Light: dig briefs — 6 repos, 110 machine-validated schemas

## Branch 3 — PEOPLE *(adoption is psychology, not procurement)*

- **Ornament: "Aim at the hated work first"** *(#8)*
  - Pilot against work people *resent* (docs, boilerplate, status reports) → wins feel like liberation, not replacement
  - Pull beats push: seed champions, demo, amnesty for experiments
  - Light: AppTrak — no mandate, one demo, "once they saw it, they were sold," 3 integrations
- **Ornament: "Verification is the new bottleneck"** *(#7)*
  - Generation scales; review doesn't. The scarce skill shifts to *specifying and verifying*
  - Delegation is the skill, not prompting: intent + constraints + acceptance criteria — train it like the leadership skill it is
  - Light: dig brief = delegation formalized (self-validating, 0 errors cold) · guardian gauntlet = review automated

## Branch 4 — PROOF *(measure, or you're just believing)*

- **Ornament: "Baseline before you buy"** *(#5)*
  - Step zero is instrumentation, not licenses — no *before*, no provable *after*
  - Pilot with a control · outcomes never activity (cycle time, escaped defects, backlog burn — NOT prompts sent)
  - Never metrics-as-surveillance: measure teams and flow, not individuals
  - Light: AppTrak IS the measurement reflex — "same discipline, applied to the delivery process itself" · bake-off verdict against your own vendor
- **Ornament: "Governance rides with the tool"** *(#9)*
  - Policy as configuration, not PDF: rules install with the tooling — compliance is the default path
  - Data boundary before scale: what enters a model, which model per sensitivity, gates on output
  - Light: 150-line auto-loaded config · `tb enlist` scaffolds rules in one command · zero SSA data has ever touched a commercial model — requirement zero, self-imposed
  - Federal hook: the posture NIST AI RMF / ATO will demand — "the formal mapping is work I want to do from the government side"

## Tinsel *(drape on any branch)*

- Every ornament closes with its evidence clause: *principle → "and I've run this in miniature: [artifact]"*
- Your tenure arc (chat → copilot → agentic, with dates) proves Terrain AND Targeting in one breath
- If they've already thought of it: "good — then we agree on the hard part," then go one level deeper

## Under the tree — DO NOT UNWRAP

- All nine at once (manifesto ≠ interview)
- The arrow (#4) more than once, or as commentary on the cuts
- "Best practices suggest…" — you have artifacts; consultants have decks

## Routing Rule

> Hear the question → **which branch?** → hang the answer on that branch's ornament → close with its evidence light.

**Two trees, one forest:** the *Framework Tree* answers "who are you?" — this tree answers "what would you do?" Panel questions about your past route to the first tree; questions about the division's future route here.
