---

## Branch 1 — BUILDER *(can he do the work?)*

- **Claim:** turns vague directives into enterprise systems; modernizes the unmodernizable
- **Ornaments:**
  - **#1** AppTrak — 20 minutes of conversation → enterprise observability platform
  - **#4** ColdFusion → .NET 6 — no debugger, no docs, no excuses
  - **#7** Dig pipeline — legacy intent recovery, machine-validated
- **Lights (numbers):** 17 apps tracked · 60+ datastores · 34K-LOC doc corpus · 110 schemas

## Branch 2 — FOLLOWER *(can he take direction?)*

- **Claim:** honest dissent before the decision, full craftsmanship after
- **Ornaments:**
  - **#3** Disagree-and-commit — recommended against, overruled, built it best-possible
  - **#8** Team of six — a 30-year architect content to execute assigned tasks
- **Kicker seed:** *"I owe leadership my recommendation before, my craftsmanship after."*

## Branch 3 — MULTIPLIER *(does his work outlive him?)*

- **Claim:** builds standards and people, then removes himself
- **Ornaments:**
  - **#2** Pipeline standard — self-taught ADO, standardized DEV/VAL/PROD, taught 3
  - **#8** Handoffs — gateway + two microservices, still running without him
  - **#4b** Mentored gov dev — .NET Core, DI, loose coupling, learned through the work
  - **AI onboarding kit** — portable config, scaffolds, plain-English explainers
- **Lights:** 3 taught → self-sufficient · 3 devs adopted AppTrak · 3 services handed off
- **Kicker seed:** *"The standard is still there; my hands aren't."*

## Branch 4 — GOVERNOR *(will he control the AI wave or just surf it?)*

- **Claim:** measures vendors, enforces invariants, guards the data boundary
- **Ornaments:**
  - **#5** Guardian gauntlet — 8 invariants, 3 subagents, enforced pre-commit
  - **#6** Bake-offs — measured selection; ruled against his own vendor
  - **Data boundary** — zero SSA code/data has ever touched a commercial model
- **Lights:** 3–5× measured velocity · 28–28 verdict, tiebreak to competitor · 304 commits / 30 days
- **Kicker seed:** *"Enforced by automation, not by remembering."*

## Routing Rule *(the whole framework in one move)*
